Before we start

A few important questions.

Can I create a realistic AI influencer like Maya Lanez?

If you mean an original virtual character with an ambitious photorealistic visual direction, that is a useful creative starting point. We would develop your own identity and test the results with a pilot—not reproduce Maya Lanez’s face, voice, brand or content.

Maya Lanez is referenced here only as a cultural example. GenieMade is independent and is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Maya Lanez or her creators. We do not claim her production process, performance or results. Visit Maya Lanez’s own links.

How much does Ultra Premium cost?

Pricing is by custom quote. The scope depends on identity development, asset count, clip duration, production complexity, revisions and usage needs. No fixed price has been announced. Standard GenieMade subscriptions and credits do not automatically include this custom service.

Is this a self-service generator?

This page starts a conversation with our team. The custom service uses human creative direction and review; it is not a promise of a new one-click influencer generator. Existing GenieMade creation tools remain available separately.

Can the avatar look and sound like me?

We can discuss an authorized personal-avatar scope. Face and voice inputs require appropriate permission and a feasibility review. Do not submit someone else’s private photos or recordings without permission. Consent to create an avatar is separate from permission to publish it.

Will the character stay identical in every scene?

Consistency is a production goal, not an unconditional guarantee. We use approved references and review outputs, then establish the acceptable range with a pilot. Complex motion, long sequences and changes in angle or lighting may need additional work or a different approach.

Can I use the output commercially?

We agree intended usage and delivery terms in your quote. Commercial use depends on the rights to supplied material, relevant model and asset licenses, and the content itself. AI generation or a provenance receipt is not automatic copyright ownership or blanket legal clearance.
