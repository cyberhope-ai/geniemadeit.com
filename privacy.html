Privacy Policy

Effective August 19, 2026 · GenieMade is a product of CyberHope AI.

This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. Questions? Email support@geniemadeit.com.

Information we collect

  • Account details — your email address and, if you choose, your name.
  • Photos you upload — images you provide to create your portraits, and the private model trained from them.
  • Mobile number — only if you choose to provide it, so we can text you when your order is ready.
  • Payment information — processed securely by Stripe; we do not store your full card number.
  • Usage data — basic logs and analytics to operate and improve the service.

How we use your information

  • To create, store, and deliver your portraits and cards, and to operate your Vault.
  • To notify you about your orders and account (by email, and by SMS if you opted in).
  • To process payments, prevent fraud, and provide support.

SMS / text messaging

We will not sell, rent, or share your mobile number or SMS opt-in consent with any third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes.

If you provide your mobile number at checkout, you consent to receive SMS text messages from GenieMade about your order and account (for example, a message when your photos are ready). Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. Reply STOP to unsubscribe at any time, or HELP for help.

Your photos and AI models

The photos you upload and the model trained from them are kept private to your account and used only to create images for you. We do not use your photos to train shared or public models. Every creation carries a tamper-evident certificate of authenticity.

Service providers

We share limited data with vendors who help us run the service, solely to perform work on our behalf: Stripe (payments), Twilio (SMS), Resend (email), and our AI image providers. They are contractually bound to protect your data.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete individual creations from your Vault, and you can request deletion of your account and associated data by emailing support@geniemadeit.com.

Children

GenieMade is intended for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Your choices

You may access, correct, or delete your information, opt out of SMS (reply STOP) or marketing email at any time. Contact us at support@geniemadeit.com.

Changes

We may update this policy; we will post the new effective date here.
