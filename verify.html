Is this real?

Deepfakes and unsigned AI images are everywhere. GenieMade takes the opposite stance: every image we create is signed, sealed, and publicly registered the moment it's made — so anyone can prove it's genuine, who created it, and exactly how. How every creation is verified

Authenticity isn't a badge we print — it's a chain of independent proofs, laid down automatically the instant your image is generated. Four layers, each one checkable on its own.

1

Content Credentials (C2PA)

Every file is stamped with tamper-evident C2PA metadata — the open industry standard from the Content Authenticity Initiative, the same "nutrition label" for media adopted across the industry. Alter the pixels and the credential breaks.

2

Sealed in your Vault (QSeal)

The creation is sealed into a PrecognitionOS Vault with a cryptographic, hash-linked chain (QSeal). Each seal references the one before it, so a record can't be altered, removed, or back-dated after the fact.

3

Registered on EverVerify

The certificate's content hash is written to EverVerify, our sister company's independent public authenticity registry — an authoritative record that stands on its own, even away from GenieMade.

4

Anchored to Bitcoin

The hash is timestamped onto the Bitcoin blockchain via OpenTimestamps — a permanent, world-verifiable proof of the exact moment your creation existed, that no one (not even us) can rewrite.

Sister company

Backed by EverVerify

EverVerify runs the independent public registry of record that stands behind every GenieMade certificate. Because it's a separate, authoritative service — not just a line in our own database — a creation's proof lives on beyond GenieMade itself: verifiable by third parties, buyers, marketplaces, platforms, and courts.

One creation, sealed in your private Vault and mirrored to a public registry anyone can query. That's the difference between claiming something is real and being able to prove it.

Why this matters

As AI images flood the internet, "is this real?" becomes the question that matters — for artists proving their work is theirs, for buyers confirming a licensed asset, for families protecting a likeness, for anyone who needs to stand behind what they share.

Provenance is the answer. A GenieMade certificate doesn't just assert that an image is authentic — it shows the receipt: when it was made, by whom, with which model and prompt, and a chain of independent proofs anyone can re-check. Authenticity you can hand to someone else, and they can confirm it without taking your word for it.
